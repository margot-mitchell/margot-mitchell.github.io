About me

I’m Margot, a post-bac researcher in Russell Poldrack’s lab in the Psychology Department at Stanford University. I completed my undergraduate degree at the University of California, Berkeley, where I studied Cognitive Science and minored in Data Science.

Since joining Russ’s lab in January 2025, much of my work has been related to an R01 validation of three cognitive constructs in NIMH’s (control, attention, and working memory). I’ve also been involved in several projects exploring interactions between AI and human cognition.

NIMH · Research Domain Criteria (RDoC)

RDoC is NIMH’s research framework for studying mental health and psychopathology as variation in basic dimensions of brain and behavioral functioning, organized into six domains, rather than starting from symptom-based diagnoses. Researchers study these dimensions from normal to abnormal, across units of analysis (from genes and circuits to behavior and self-report), development, and environment, to inform better assessment, diagnosis, and treatment.

See the RDoC Matrix on the NIMH website
