Interests

What does control failure look like at the computational level, and how upstream is it?

  • When a person fails to take an action they know they should take, or fails to inhibit an action they know they shouldn’t, is this a failure of capacity, or motivation, or some amalgamation of the two?
  • How do lower-level control processes, captured by task parameters such as drift rate, relate to how an individual applies behavioral control in their everyday life?
  • Is control a global capacity, or largely domain-specific?
  • How trait- or state-like is control capacity? How much does context matter? How much variation is there, between people, in the stability of control? How much of it is learned? What supports or hinders that learning?
  • Is control too broad a concept to be scientifically useful? Is the assumption of control as a single, coherent thing just a hindrance to progress in the field at this point?
  • How does control fit within research frameworks like RDoC and HiTOP, and how can it be used to reconcile those dimensional ontologies with categorical ones, such as the DSM?
