I am interested in behavioral control and decision-making. Specifically, I want to understand the forces that influence how people fail to align their behavior and goals: How upstream is the failure? What does it look like at a computational level? How does it break down in maladaptive behavior? To study these processes, I use a combination of computational methods, behavioral experiments, and neuroimaging.

Keywords: cognitive control; decision-making; psychometrics; computational modeling; behavioral change
